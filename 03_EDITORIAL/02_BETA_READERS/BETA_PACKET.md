# BETA READER PACKET: SOMATIC CANTICLES

## 1. Welcome & Guidelines

Thank you for agreeing to read *Somatic Canticles*. Your feedback is critical in helping us refine this story.

**The "Black Box" Rule**:
Please be honest. If a section is boring, confusing, or annoying, tell us. You don't need to fix it—just tell us *where* you felt it properly. "I was bored on page 42" is more valuable than "You should rewrite Chapter 3."

**The Specific Lens**:
We are particularly interested in:
1.  **Readability**: The book uses a lot of made-up technical terms ("Khalorēē", "NOESIS"). Did you feel lost? Did context clues help you understand them, or did you need a glossary constantly?
2.  **Emotional Connection**: Did you care about the Somanaut team?
3.  **The "Woo-Woo" Factor**: This is a sci-fi book about spirituality. Does the system feel grounded and rigorous, or simply like magic?

## 2. Beta Reader Questionnaire

### General Impressions
*   What was your favorite scene? Why?
*   What was your least favorite scene? Why?
*   At what point (if any) did you put the book down and struggle to pick it back up?
*   Did the ending (Book 3) feel earned?

### Characters
*   **Dr. Corvan Singh**: Did you understand his motivation? Did he feel too passive?
*   **Dr. Sona Rey**: Did she feel like a distinct character, or just "the emotional one"?
*   **Dr. Jian Li**: Was his skepticism annoying or relatable?
*   **The Gardener**: Did you understand what this entity was trying to do? Did you fear it?

### World & Terminology
*   **NOESIS / Khalorēē**: Were these terms consistent? Did you get a "sense" of what they meant even if you couldn't define them perfectly?
*   **The Vine**: Did the metaphor of the Vine (determinism) land for you?
*   **Bio-Punk Aesthetic**: How did you visualize the technology? (e.g., "Meat computers" vs "Glowing energy")

### Plot & Pacing
*   **Book 1**: Was the intro too slow?
*   **Book 3**: Did the "Severance Event" make sense, or was it too abstract?

### The "Aletheos" Question
*   The central theme is about waking up from a "programmed" reality. Did this theme resonate with you personally?
