# DEVELOPMENTAL EDITING: CHARACTER ARC ANALYSIS

**Objective**: Ensure the emotional and philosophical integrity of the Somanaut team's journey across the trilogy. This document serves as a "checklist" for editing the character beats.

## 1. DR. CORVAN LUMINTH (The Peacemaker -> The Magus)
**Core Vector**: *Authorship*

*   **Book 1 Check**: Does Corvan spend at least 60% of the book *reacting* to the Anamnesis Engine? The shift to *initiation* in the climax must feel forced upon him, a reluctant assumption of power.
    *   *Key Scene*: The moment he decides to "re-write" the script in the Engine (Ch 8). Is his hesitation palpable?
*   **Book 2 Check**: Is he holding space or trying to fix things? The error of the "Peacekeeper" is to premature smoothing. Ensure he fails at least once by trying to calm a necessary conflict.
*   **Book 3 Check**: The "Refusal of Meaning" in Chapter 27 (Dialogue with The Gardener). This must be his strongest moment.
    *   *Audit*: Does he articulate *why* meaning can be a trap? Ensure he doesn't sound nihilistic, but transcendent.

## 2. DR. SONA VIRETH (The Individualist -> The High Priestess)
**Core Vector**: *Resonance*

*   **Book 1 Check**: The "Emotional Contagion" needs to be visceral. When she gets overwhelmed, it should affect the physical environment (lights flickering, bio-readings spiking).
*   **Book 2 Check**: The shift from *victim of empathy* to *master of resonance*. Look for the scene where she feels pain but doesn't drown in it.
    *   *Key Scene*: The Chorus interaction (Ch 13). She needs to conduct the pain, not just absorb it.
*   **Book 3 Check**: The "Note of Joy." This is the hardest arc to land without being cheesy. The joy must feel earned through the suffering of previous books.
    *   *Audit*: Is her rejection of "Perfect Peace" convincing? Why would an empath reject a world without pain? (Answer: Because it lacks depth).

## 3. DR. JIAN QUORIL (The Investigator -> The Emperor)
**Core Vector**: *Logic/System*

*   **Book 1 Check**: His skepticism must be grounded, not annoying. He is the audience surrogate for the materialist worldview. Give him "wins" where his logic saves the day, so his eventual failure (where logic fails) hits harder.
*   **Book 2 Check**: The "Resonance Topology." Ensure his "technobabble" evolves. He should start using poetic metaphors *as* technical terms.
*   **Book 3 Check**: "I don't want to be correct; I want to be alive."
    *   *Audit*: This line (or sentiment) needs to be the climax of his arc. Verify it appears or is thematically represented in the Severance sequence.

## 4. GIDEON SETER (The Challenger -> The Chariot)
**Core Vector**: *Defense/Structure*

*   **Book 1 Check**: Gideon needs to physically intervene. Show, don't tell, his protective instinct.
*   **Book 3 Check**: The hardest choice for a protector is to let his charges take a risk that might kill them.
    *   *Audit*: In the final procedure (Ch 24), does he *almost* abort the mission? The tension of him holding back his instinct to save them is crucial.

## 5. THE GARDENER (Antagonist)
**Core Vector**: *Conservation/Determinism*

*   **Nuance Check**: The Gardener must NEVER sneer or act "villainous." It should act like a concerned parent or a rigorous scientist. It truly believes the Vine is the only safe place for consciousness.
*   **Audit**: Check every line of dialogue in Book 3. Remove any "evil gloating." Replace with "sorrowful necessity."

---

## 6. PACING & STAKES CHEAT SHEET

*   **Book 1**: Mystery / Horror / Discovery. *Pacing: Slow burn -> Rapid disintegration.*
*   **Book 2**: Tragedy / Opera / Depth. *Pacing: Wavelike, emotional swells.*
*   **Book 3**: Thriller / Metaphysical Action. *Pacing: Ticking clock -> Explosion -> Silence.*
