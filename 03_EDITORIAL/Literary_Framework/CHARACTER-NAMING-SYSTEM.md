## I. The Naming Philosophy

In the *Somatic Canticles* universe, a name is not merely a label—it is a **vibrational key**. The naming system utilizes five layers of resonance to ensure narrative immortality:

1. **Surface**: Memorable and phonetically pleasing.

4. **Archetypal**: Alignment with Enneagram growth/stress patterns.

## III. Subconscious Layering Strategy

### 1. Sound Symbolism (Phonosemantics)
- **Hard Consonants (K, T, P, G)**: Used for grounding and defensive archetypes. 
    - *Example*: **G**ideon (Reptilian mastery).
- **Sibilants and Liquids (S, L, M, N)**: Used for emotional and fluid archetypes. 
    - *Example*: **S**o**n**a (Limbic flow).
- **Vocalic Openness**: Used for expansive and mental archetypes.
    - *Example*: Corve**a**n (Witness expansion).

### 2. The Archetypal "Hidden" Meaning
- **Corvan**: Derived from "Corvus" (Crow/Raven) - The psychopomp/messenger between worlds (Witness).

- **Jian**: Derived from "Sword" or "Strength" (East Asian roots) - The clinical analytical edge.
- **Gideon**: Derived from "Hewer" or "Great Warrior" (Biblical roots) - The protector of the foundation.
