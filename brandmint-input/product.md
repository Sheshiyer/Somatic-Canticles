# Somatic Canticles — Product MD

## Product Name

Somatic Canticles

## Category

Premium three-book hard science fiction trilogy spanning consciousness literature, trauma-healing narrative, and philosophical SF.

## Overview

Somatic Canticles is a trilogy where consciousness engineering is rendered with the precision of PubMed journals and the visionary artistry of sacred anatomy. The project bridges hard neuroscience, somatic trauma healing, consciousness philosophy, and Tarot-biological symbolism inside an epic narrative structure.

## Core Proposition

If Peter Watts wrote *The Body Keeps the Score* as visionary fiction.

## Audience

### Persona

The Philosopher-Healer: a 28-48 year old knowledge worker, therapist, scientist, engineer, writer, or consciousness explorer who refuses the false binary between rigorous science and embodied mysticism.

### What They Want

- Hard SF rigor without emotional coldness
- Trauma-healing narrative without clinical flatness
- Biological mechanisms without vague new-age language
- Visionary imagery without sacrificing intellectual depth

## Value Proposition

### Functional Value

Provides vocabulary and frameworks for consciousness engineering grounded in biology, including trauma neuroscience, polyvagal theory, cardiac coherence, and embodied transformation.

### Emotional Value

Makes trauma healing feel epic rather than clinical. Validates the reader's desire for rigor and embodiment to coexist.

### Self-Expressive Value

Signals that the reader refuses the science-versus-mysticism binary and identifies with the Philosopher-Healer archetype.

### Social Value

Creates shared language across hard SF readers, trauma practitioners, meditation communities, and philosophy-of-mind audiences.

## Differentiators

- High rigor + high embodiment + transformational arc
- Tarot Major Arcana mapped to specific biological events across 22 chapters
- Trilogy depth instead of short-form philosophical thought experiments
- Clinical dialogue paired with visionary, sensory prose
- Aletheia vs. Lethe philosophical framework grounded in body-based transformation

## Trilogy Lineup
