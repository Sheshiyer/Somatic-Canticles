# SWA-025 Contract: Provenance Traceability Scoring Guide

## Score Anchors (0-5)
- `0`: no traceable source evidence
- `1`: source present but ambiguous
- `2`: source present, weak evidence linkage
- `3`: source and evidence link mostly clear
- `4`: strong source/evidence alignment
- `5`: complete and auditable lineage

## Evidence
- Every score must link to `source_path` and `provenance_ref`.
