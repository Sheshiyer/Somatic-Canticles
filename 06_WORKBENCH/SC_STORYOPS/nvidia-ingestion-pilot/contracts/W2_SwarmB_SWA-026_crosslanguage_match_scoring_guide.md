# SWA-026 Contract: Cross-Language Match Scoring Guide

## Score Anchors (0-5)
- `0`: cross-language retrieval fails consistently
- `1`: sparse matches with major semantic drift
- `2`: partial alignment with notable noise
- `3`: generally aligned with occasional drift
- `4`: strong semantic alignment across language variants
- `5`: robust and stable multilingual alignment
