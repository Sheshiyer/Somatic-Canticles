# Naming Conventions

## Files
- Pattern: `{artifact_name}_{YYYY-MM-DD}.{ext}` for dated reports
- Stable artifacts may remain suffix-free if they are canonical pilot surfaces

## IDs
- Nodes: `NODE-001` to `NODE-999`
- Queries: `Q001` to `Q999`
- Tasks: `SWA-001` to `SWA-999`
- Risks: `R-01` to `R-99`

## Batch IDs
- `BATCH-{bucket}-{date}-{seq}`
- Example: `BATCH-biofield-2026-05-15-01`

## Model Run IDs
- `RUN-{model_short}-{phase}-{timestamp}`
- Example: `RUN-bge-m3-embed-20260515T1033`
