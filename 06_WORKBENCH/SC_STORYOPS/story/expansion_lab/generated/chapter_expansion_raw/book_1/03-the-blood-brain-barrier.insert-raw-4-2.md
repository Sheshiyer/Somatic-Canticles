Sona’s fingers flick across the haptic cuff on her wrist, silencing the arterial pulse alarm she herself tripped two heartbeats ago. “Field protocol says we titrate 250 mL of buffered lactate, then sit tight for four minutes. I say we skip the four minutes and trust the ginger.”

Sona meets his eyes, tired but steady. “We already did that once. Paper doesn’t suture silence.”
