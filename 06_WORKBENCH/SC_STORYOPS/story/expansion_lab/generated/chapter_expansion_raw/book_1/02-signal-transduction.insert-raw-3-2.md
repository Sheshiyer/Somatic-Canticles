Corv paused at the lip of the funnel, one hand on the frame as though testing a pulse. “Protocol says we owe the archive one more checksum,” he said, voice low, almost bantering. “If we leave without it, the breach might reflex-seal on Jian’s trace. Library likes to keep souvenirs.”

Gideon lifted the blade a fraction, letting the green seam paint a stripe across Corv’s cheek. “You volunteering to stay behind and tick the box, old man? Thought you’d had enough of unpaid overtime.”

Jian flicked ash from an imaginary pipe into the pocket of air that still tasted of benzoin. “We could send the amber,” he offered. “Let it race the shear and come back pregnant with entropy. Simpler.”

Sona’s answer arrived before any of them moved: a soft click, like a thumbnail against glass. The copper coin spun on the armrest once, then settled head-up—crow looking left. “One checksum,” she said. “But the witness clause says I have to carry the measure.” She tucked a loose strand behind her ear, the motion making her outline fuzz for half a heartbeat. “I’ll walk the margin with you three, then peel off. Net time cost: twenty-two seconds. Acceptable?”

Corv exhaled through his teeth, measuring the risk against the boredom of later regret. “Fine. On three, we step in phase. No heroics, no lag. If the membrane stutters, we all back out.” He glanced at Gideon. “Even you, knight.”

Gideon shrugged, the gesture almost graceful. “Not here for heroics. Just here to keep the hole the right shape.” He tapped the sword’s spine twice; the green seam pulsed, once, twice, like a consent heartbeat.

Jian lifted the amber between thumb and forefinger, letting the light fractal through its core. “I’ll key it to my pulse. When the amber blacks out, we’re one second from shear collapse. Last chance to abort.”

They formed a loose crescent, shoulders almost brushing, breath syncing without ceremony. Sona slipped the copper coin into Corv’s palm—payment deferred, not forgiven. He curled his fingers around it, surprised by its residual warmth.

“Three,” Corv said, and they stepped forward together.
