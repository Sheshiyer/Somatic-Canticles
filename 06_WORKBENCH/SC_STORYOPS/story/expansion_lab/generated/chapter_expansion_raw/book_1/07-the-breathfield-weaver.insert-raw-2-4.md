Corv allowed a thin smile to brush his lips. “That secondary oscillation is the field’s own corrective feedback. It’s the signature of a system that refuses to be a static diagram.”

Sona let the word “debrief” settle over her like a thin coat of ash. She already knew which detail would haunt her: the moment she had tried to rescue the field into silence. As they walked the corridor’s gradual incline toward the lift, Sona felt the residual resonance leave her joints in small, pleasant aftershocks. “What?” she asked, her voice breaking the quiet.

“I was wondering,” he said, “whether you still taste the guest breath on the back of your tongue.”
