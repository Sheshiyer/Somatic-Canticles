# Book 1 Workbench

This folder now stages the first downstream Book 1 scaffolding pass because the trilogy intake layer has produced a defensible assignment spine.

Current scope:

- starter packet scaffolding remains the default
- active `working/` chapter copies: `Chapter 01-05`
- the diagnostic lane is fully in prose-pass territory, and the patterned-captivity lane is now live through `Chapter 05`

Book 1 spine:

- diagnostic architecture and catastrophic rejection: Chapters `01-03`
- patterned captivity and inherited doctrine: Chapters `04-06`
- breath, orientation, and calibration: Chapters `07-08`

Use this folder when:

- the relevant source and image bundles are stable enough to justify Book 1 ownership
- a packet can reduce uncertainty without pretending the prose pass has already started
- the chapter's concepts remain anchored to the trilogy intake layer rather than local intuition

Rules:

1. Use `packet_board.md` before opening or revising any packet.
2. Do not create a `working/` chapter copy until the corresponding packet is ready for an active prose pass.
3. Every packet must cite:
   - the chapter source,
   - the compiled Book 1 reference,
   - the concept/projection surfaces,
   - and at least one image or motif support family when the chapter is image-bearing.
4. Use `intake/world_bible_authority_registry.md` to keep world-bible claims in bounds.
5. A future merge-back must compare any staged rewrite against `02_MANUSCRIPTS/COMPILED/Book_1_Anamnesis_Engine.md`, not only against the source chapter file.

Layout:

- `packet_board.md`: chapter-to-spine assignment and scaffold posture
- `packets/`: starter packet files
- `working/`: active chapter copies only after a packet is promoted into prose work
