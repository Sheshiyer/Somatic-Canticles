# Trilogy Intake Layer

This folder is the `v0.2` upgrade surface for `SC_STORYOPS`.

It exists to prepare existing material before assigning new pressure to any single book. The job here is not to generate prose. The job is to make the trilogy's research, image intelligence, worldbuilding, and concept architecture usable inside the InkOS-shaped `story/` framework.

## Sequence

1. Lock contradictions in `../clarify_log.md`.
2. Extract high-signal vault domains in `vault_source_domains.md`.
3. Register reusable concepts and principles in `concept_registry.md`.
4. Sort lore and worldbuilding by authority level in `lore_worldbuilding_map.md`.
5. Convert external images into a principle layer in `image_principles.md`.
6. Use `book_projection_board.md` to decide whether anything should be assigned to Book 1, Book 2, Book 3, or remain cross-trilogy.

## Non-Goals

- not a raw archive dump
- not a replacement canon
- not direct drafting into `COMPILED/`
- not forcing every concept into a chapter before the support layer is ready

## Output Standard

Every intake artifact should condense, point, and justify. It should not fork canon accidentally.

# Image Principles

## Purpose

This file captures what the image layer is actually teaching the trilogy.

The goal is not to archive every screenshot. The goal is to extract reusable narrative principles from image families so visual intelligence can enter the workbench as structure.

## Current Principle Families

| Family | Principle | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Witness gradients | Consciousness must be discriminated by level, not treated as one glowing abstraction | protects the trilogy from flattening witness, awareness, and intelligence into the same register |
| Cardiac resonance | Coherence is embodied interval and measurable relation, not sentiment | keeps heart language physiological and field-based |
| Pain as information | Diagnostic contact requires signal respect, not premature soothing | stabilizes debug scenes and clinical compassion |
| Developmental mutation | Growth is continuity through transformation | supports identity change without purity ideology |
| Deception layers | Distortion operates across scales at once | useful for antagonism, governance, and false-order systems |

## Extraction Workflow

1. Observe the image literally.
2. Name the principle it implies.
3. Link the principle to a biological or conceptual hook.
4. Link that hook to a story need.
5. Leave projection to books soft until the support converges.

## Anti-Drift Rule

- Do not use images as decoration.
- Do not use images as mystical mood boards.
- Use them as compressed arguments that can sharpen scenes, systems, and world logic.
