## Book 2 Pressure Notes

- `Ch. 9` should deepen Gideon's shift from defense to foundation.
- `Ch. 10` should deepen Corv's move from interpreter to disciplined witness.
- `Ch. 11` should show that mutation is not self-betrayal.
- `Ch. 12` should frame restart as operational honesty, not reset fantasy.
- `Ch. 13` should make relation feel risky, exact, and earned.
- `Ch. 14` should show inner governance rather than abstract balance.
- `Ch. 15` should end with threshold, not triumphal completion.

## Emotional Continuity Checks

- No one should sound spiritually solved in Book 2.
- Book 2 gains must read as usable under pressure, not final transcendence.
- Book 3 temptations must feel custom-built from each character's earlier compensations.
