# Book 3 Compiled Surface Audit

## Purpose

This file audits the current compiled `Book 3` manuscript surface against the active trilogy rules before any packet scaffolding opens.

The target is not to rewrite `Book 3` here. The target is to identify the highest-risk compiled drift so later packet or prose work does not inherit stale vocabulary, flattened dialogue, sermonizing, or an over-mechanized Gardener.

## Audit Verdict

- The large-scale cleanup is partially holding: the compiled surface no longer carries the worst old-name and placeholder failures.
- The main remaining problems are not broad canon collapse. They are local but structurally important:
  - stale compiled scaffolding still embedded in chapter bodies
  - Chapter `18` voice flattening and doctrine-heavy explanation
  - unstable witness and vector lexicon
  - a Gardener that often reads as mechanism or cosmology before it reads as conservational intelligence
  - post-Severance prose drifting into manifesto register instead of staying biologically anchored

## Category Review

### Vocabulary

#### Holding clean

- No `Singh / Rey / Li / Vance` carryover was found.
- No `Chetana Yantra`, `Crystallization Interface protocols`, `Khalorēē Field Architecture`, or loose `quantum systems` residue was found.

#### Active drift

- `consciousness vessel` and `consciousness vessels` still appear where the style surface prefers `witness vessel`.
- `Manas Yantra` still appears once as a local canon slip; the canon term is `Manas Interface`.
- The observer lexicon is unstable across:
  - `self-consciousness`
  - `Witness Awareness`
  - `Witness Lens`
  - `Witness State`
  - `Witness Capacity`
- The three-vector lexicon is also unstable across:
  - `Pure Joy`
  - `Catalyst Event`
  - `Catalyst Clarity`
  - `Present Coherence`
  - older vector handles such as `Soma Vector` and `Muladhara Terminus`
- `Salik level Khalorēē protocols` reads like unresolved terminology instead of the current cleanup plan's culture-specific phrasing.

### Role-Bound Dialogue

- The largest drift zone is `Chapter 18`, where the four leads often speak in the same polished metaphysical register.
- Sona drifts furthest when the language becomes universalized doctrine rather than sensory/empathic precision.
- Jian drifts when analytic language gives way to generalized threshold wisdom instead of procedural exactness.
- Gideon drifts when protective/structural language becomes abstract becoming-language rather than boundary, passage, or holding logic.
- The temptation chapters are more differentiated; the synthesis and late-book material is where voice convergence becomes risky.

### Anti-Sermon Posture

- `Chapter 18` is the clearest anti-sermon failure zone:
  - repeated `not X, but Y` explanation blocks
  - vector-definition discourse
  - high-author summary language instead of dramatized somatic consequence
- `Chapter 20-21` still pause too often to explain theme instead of letting procedure and pressure carry it.
- `Chapter 25-27` push hardest into manifesto or graduation rhetoric instead of close-third embodied making.
- Embedded compiled-surface overlays also act like sermonizers by themselves:
  - `RESONANCE PROFILE`
  - `Narrative Function`
  - raw diagnostic or protocol blocks

### Gardener Conservational Voice

- No sneering, gloating, or cartoon-villain failure was found.
- The miss is different:
  - the Gardener is often explained as `system`, `universe`, `gravity`, `homeostasis`, or `maintenance algorithm`
  - the prose frequently narrates around the Gardener's function instead of dramatizing sorrowful necessity through encounter or speech
- The result is over-mechanization:
  - the Gardener remains conceptually correct
  - but its conservational motive is too often paraphrased rather than voiced

## Highest-Risk Compiled Deltas

1. Embedded production scaffolding still lives inside the compiled manuscript surface.
Anchor phrases:
- `RESONANCE PROFILE`
- `Narrative Function`
- raw source or diagnostic blocks

2. `Chapter 18` is the single densest exposition-collapse zone.
Anchor phrases:
- `The Synthesis Protocol was complete`
- extended vector-definition and planning discourse

3. The Gardener is over-mechanized into cosmology or systems-theory language.
Anchor phrases:
- `It's a law of nature`
- `the mechanism was the universe`
- `homeostasis`
- `patience of gravity`

4. Local canon still slips in the compiled surface.
Anchor phrases:
- `Manas Yantra`
- `consciousness vessel`
- unstable witness and vector naming

5. Post-Severance chapters drift from `PubMed x Alex Grey` into generalized metaphysical manifesto.
Anchor phrases:
- `They had escaped nothing. They had graduated into everything.`
- `The sky was the color of a promise`
- strong creation-manifesto passages in `25-27`

## Safe Consequence

- `Book 3` should remain packet-closed.
- `16-18` may still stay source-mapped, but `Chapter 18` is now the clearest compiled hotspot that later packet work must not inherit uncritically.
- `22-24` and `25-27` should not open until the later delta ledger extracts the highest-risk drift into an actionable cleanup sequence.
