## Consolidated Safe Claims

### Governance and captivity

- The maintained order can be modeled as a civilizational-scale preservation system rather than a single villain.
- House or faction pressures may continue to act as delayed instruments of the Vine.
- Emergency-law or preservation rhetoric can support the late-trilogy false-mercy logic if translated into current vocabulary first.

### Authored reality

- Post-Severance life should feel like continuous creation under responsibility, not like arrival at a static utopia.
- Differentiated forces must remain differentiated inside a coherent order; do not collapse `Book 3` into merger mysticism.
- Cellular and symbolic governance metaphors are valid as design pressure, but not yet as hard canon mechanics.

### Structural lie and deception

- Distortion is multi-level: naming, memory, tactical framing, institutional maintenance, and inherited narrative law.
- `Book 3` should reveal captivity as administered order, not only personal trauma.
- False worlds should tempt by competence, peace, explanation, and predictability.

## Claims Still Blocked

- Exact legal structure of the Federation as on-page canon
- Fully specified post-Severance social architecture
- Any direct import of stale house titles, ranks, or obsolete vessel terminology
- Any scene claim that depends on a singleton image family without corroborating support

- `Chapter 25-27` authored-reality design pressure
