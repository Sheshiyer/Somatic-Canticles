## Non-Negotiable Book 3 Constraints

### End-goal continuity

- `Book 1` established readable captivity.
- `Book 2` established resonant self-consciousness without merger.
- `Book 3` must therefore move from liberation pressure into authorship practice, not reset into generic cosmic exposition.

### Gardener posture

- The Gardener preserves order because it prefers stable harvest, not because it enjoys cruelty.
- The Gardener may tempt, redefine, maintain, or prune; it should not rant, sneer, or collapse into cartoon antagonism.

### Liberation logic

- Severance is not force, revenge, or conquest.
- Ripening is developmental completion.
- The team exits determinism by holding the vectors exactly enough for a natural disconnection.
- Authorship begins after Severance; it is not a pre-earned identity badge.

### Character-specific refusals

- Corv rejects teleological false mercy and holds Catalyst Clarity.
- Sona rejects anesthesia disguised as peace and holds Pure Joy.
- Jian rejects the cage of total explanation and must stay alive to uncertainty.
- Gideon rejects static safety and defends passage rather than prison walls.

### Language and process continuity

- Keep Somanaut dialogue role-bound and clinical.
- Keep narration visionary but biologically anchored.
- Do not use Book `3` to reintroduce deprecated compounds, sermonizing, or premature mastery language.
