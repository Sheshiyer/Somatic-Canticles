# Trilogy Intake Layer

This folder is the `v0.2` upgrade surface for `SC_STORYOPS`.

It exists to prepare existing material before assigning new pressure to any single book. The job here is not to generate prose. The job is to make the trilogy's research, image intelligence, worldbuilding, and concept architecture usable inside the InkOS-shaped `story/` framework.

## Sequence

1. Lock contradictions in `../clarify_log.md`.
2. Extract high-signal vault domains in `vault_source_domains.md`.
3. Register reusable concepts and principles in `concept_registry.md`.
4. Sort lore and worldbuilding by authority level in `lore_worldbuilding_map.md`.
5. Convert external images into a principle layer in `image_principles.md`.
6. Register broader Noesis image clusters in `noesis_image_cluster_registry.md`.
7. Register adjacent synthesis articles in `blog_article_registry.md`.
8. Use `book_projection_board.md` to decide whether anything should be assigned to Book 1, Book 2, Book 3, or remain cross-trilogy.

## Non-Goals

- not a raw archive dump
- not a replacement canon
- not direct drafting into `COMPILED/`
- not forcing every concept into a chapter before the support layer is ready

## Output Standard

Every intake artifact should condense, point, and justify. It should not fork canon accidentally.
