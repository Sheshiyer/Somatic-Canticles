# Synchronocities Blog Article Registry

## Purpose

This registry treats `01-Projects/synchronocities-blog/` as an explicit intake source for `SC_STORYOPS`.

The blog is not canon. It is a synthesis surface: an adjacent corpus that compresses vault-scale research into article-sized architectures, diagnostic frames, and visual handles. Use it to sharpen principles and translation layers, not to overrule trilogy doctrine.

The original Synchronocities project path is not mounted in this checkout. Treat this file as the local handle registry for that source family until the external corpus is restored.

## Use Rules

- Treat blog posts as secondary synthesis, not primary canon.
- Use a blog post when it makes an upstream source family more legible or gives a clearer systems-language handle for a concept already emerging in the vault.
- If the blog introduces a stronger claim than the underlying project/world-bible/resource sources can support, mark the concept `soft` or `review-required`.
- Prefer the blog when you need:
  - a compact architecture description,
  - a diagnostic stack,
  - or a visual concept handle that compresses a larger source lattice.

## Current Best-Fit Contribution

The blog is strongest where the trilogy needs clean translation between:

- research abundance and narrative usability,
- symbolic language and measurable systems,
- and image-rich intuition and editorially controllable concept maps.
