## Purpose

This board keeps concept-to-book mapping evidence-based without pretending the trilogy is ready for hard lock everywhere.

Use this board to answer:

- which book currently owns a material family most strongly
- which books only seed or carry it
- what evidence actually supports the placement
- whether the material is ready for downstream packeting or still needs intake work

## Placement Rule

- `primary-high` means the evidence strongly supports one book as the dominant owner, but downstream packeting can still wait.
- `primary-medium-high` means the placement is likely correct, but a missing authority layer still blocks a hard lock.
- `cross-spine-high` means the family is genuinely trilogy-wide and should not be artificially monopolized by one book.
- `bridge-medium-high` means the family links two books and should be staged as a transfer function rather than a silo.
