# Book 3 Delta Ledger

## Purpose

This ledger converts the completed `Book 3` compiled-surface audit and entry carryover validation into an ordered downstream decision surface.

It is not a prose-edit checklist yet. It is the staging ledger that decides:

- which compiled deltas are dangerous enough to block packet opening
- which deltas can be tolerated only if a packet opens with explicit constraints
- which chapter clusters must remain closed regardless of stronger image or source support

## Ordering Rule

- Lower number = earlier decision pressure on `Issue 13`
- A delta is `packet-blocking` when opening a packet before constraining that drift would simply re-import the compiled problem
- A delta is `packet-conditional` when a packet may open only if the packet contract explicitly fences the drift
- A delta is `projection-holding` when the affected cluster should remain closed even after earlier deltas are acknowledged

## Delta Notes

### `01` Compiled scaffolding contamination

- This is the most basic blocking delta because it corrupts the reading surface itself.
- Future packets must distinguish:
  - canonical story body
  - compiled-era production overlay
  - later intake interpretation

### `02` Chapter 18 synthesis collapse

- This is the first real reason `16-18` cannot be treated as automatically safe just because the source map is ready.
- `Chapter 16` and `17` are more salvageable at packet level.
- `Chapter 18` is the cluster hotspot that can still infect the whole lane with explanation voice.

### `03` Entry-hinge softness

- This delta comes from the carryover validation rather than the compiled-audit pass alone.
- The issue is not that the trilogy logic is missing.
- The issue is that the hinge currently relies on recap and soft merged-field language instead of explicit threshold inheritance.

### `04` Gardener macro-lore inflation

- This is the main guard against loose macro-lore entering both the reveal and the temptation bands.
- It is also the main reason `17` cannot be treated as a simple villain-reveal packet.

### `05` Lexicon instability

- Lower risk than the structural deltas above, but still dangerous because it muddies procedure.
- This is especially important if `19-21` ever opens later, since triangulation needs exact naming.

### `06` False-perfection / structural-lie governance overreach

- This is the clearest `22-24` hold line.
- Source support is not the problem anymore.
- The problem is that the compiled register can still over-explain the maintained order and flatten the temptations into thesis scenes.

### `07` Premature mastery after Severance

- This is the clearest `25-27` hold line.
- Authored reality is not yet safe to stage as a packet lane because the compiled prose still outruns hard-authority corroboration and overstates completion.

## Decision Reading For `Issue 13`

- `16-18`: may be considered for starter packets only if `02`, `03`, `04`, and `05` are accepted as explicit packet constraints.
- `19-21`: do not open yet through this ledger alone; they remain soft until the packet-opening decision has already proven the `16-18` lane can hold the rules.
- `22-24`: remain packet-closed.
- `25-27`: remain projection-heavy.

## Safe Consequence

- The ledger does not authorize full `Book 3` opening.
- It does make the next decision sharper:
  - either `13` keeps the freeze fully intact
  - or `13` opens only `16-18` starter packets under explicit delta constraints
- No later cluster should open from this state.
