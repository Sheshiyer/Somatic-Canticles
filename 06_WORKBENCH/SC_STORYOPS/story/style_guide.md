# SC_STORYOPS Style Guide

## Purpose

This is the condensed prose contract for workbench-stage writing and revision prep.

## Narration

- Clinical precision is non-negotiable when naming body systems, processes, and states.
- Visionary scale is earned through sensory rendering, not through generic mysticism.
- The scene should carry the system. Avoid stopping to explain what the scene can dramatize.
- The body is not symbolic wallpaper; physiology is part of the argument.
- Book 3 is the tonal ceiling: no soft onboarding, no coy archive theater, no reader coddling.

## Dialogue

- Dialogue is more clinical than narration.
- The Somanauts are professionals. Even awe comes through method.
- Distinct voice matters more than decorative line-level beauty.
- Use subtext, interruption, and action beats to prevent explanation-heavy exchanges.
- If a line could be said by any of the four leads, it is not ready.

## Noesis Calibration

- Grounded
- Direct
- Respectful-challenging
- Structural, not ornamental, use of Kha-Ba-La and related canon terms

## Banned Drift

- Generic uplift
- Wellness copy disguised as narration
- "the process is the author" framing
- explanation blocks that flatten tension
- legacy register that re-centers obsolete shells, old domains, or stale system names

## Source Use

- Every future chapter pass must identify which claims are carried by:
  - project canon,
  - world-bible canon,
  - research sources,
  - image logic.
- If source support is thin, mark the packet instead of improvising certainty.

## Preferred Revision Sequence

1. Clarify the trilogy-level principle or concept being carried.
2. Confirm the source support.
3. Confirm the image or motif support.
4. Confirm the lore/worldbuilding authority level.
5. Confirm whether the material is actually ready for book assignment.
6. Confirm dialogue voice ownership.
7. Only then revise prose.
