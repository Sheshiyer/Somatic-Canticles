## Tagging Contract

Every image that becomes usable in this workbench should eventually capture:

- file path
- observed motif
- extracted principle
- biological hook
- lore or governance hook
- possible Brandmint family link
- projection status: `unassigned`, `soft`, or `locked`
- if the image comes from `synchronocities-blog`, the article slug or cluster that gives it interpretive context

## Projection Rule

- Do not assign an image family to a book or chapter just because it feels adjacent.
- First align it to a concept in `intake/concept_registry.md`.
- Then let `intake/book_projection_board.md` determine whether the fit is real enough to stage downstream.
- Blog-derived images are compression aids, not replacement canon; pair them with project, world-bible, or research support before downstream use.
