# Artifact Protocols

This directory catalogs the physical and digital artifacts that exist both within the Somatic Canticles universe and as real-world extensions of the NOESIS system.

## Decision Mirrors

**In-World**: Advanced interfaces or pools (like the Oracle's pool) where characters can see the probability of their choices reflected against their internal coherence.
**Real-World**: Financial and practical decision-making tools provided by NOESIS.
*Function*: To reflect internal states and illuminate risk windows without making the decision for the user.

## Symbolic Narratives (Visual Systems)

**In-World**: "Glyph-Streams" or "Holographic Parables".
**Real-World**: Manga/Illustrated formats of the lore.
*Function*: Bypasses intellectual resistance to speak directly to pattern recognition.

## The Infinite Treasure Hunt

**In-World**: The "Great Game" or "The Path of the Hidden Cipher" played by the Houses.
**Real-World**: A cryptographic game embedded in the text.
*Function*: A long-form engagement that rewards deep reading and sustained inquiry. The "Treasure" is the internal compass developed during the search.

## Ritual Objects

**In-World**: "Focus Totems", "Somatic Anchors", "Harmonic Tuners".
**Real-World**: Physical merchandise (blends, tools, coins).
*Function*: To ground abstract inquiry in material reality.
