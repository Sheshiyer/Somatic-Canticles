# Meta-Narrative System

This directory details the mechanics that bridge the gap between the fiction and the reader's reality. It defines how the "Somatic Canticles" functions not just as a story, but as a "Living System" engaged by the reader.

## The Somatic Engagement Model

The trilogy is designed as a **Somatic Canticle**: a narrative system where content unfolds based on biorhythms and cognitive cycles. Note: In a static book format, this is simulated through narrative structure (Pacing) or via the digital companion experience.

### Function
- **Rhythm**: The story moves in waves of intensity and rest, mimicking the breath and nervous system regulation.
- **Integration Pauses**: The narrative structures "gaps" or "silences"—periods where the reader is encouraged to stop and integrate, rather than binge-read.

## Witness Agents (Internal Guardrails)

In the fiction, **Witness Agents** may be manifested as:
- **AI Companions / Daemons**: Specific constructs that accompany Somanauts to ensure they don't lose themselves in the Void.
- **Narrative Voices**: The "Narrator" that breaks the fourth wall to check in on the reader's state.
- **Function**: To act as pillars of coherence, preventing the "Somanaut" (Reader/Character) from being overwhelmed by symbolic depth.

## The Reader as Somanaut

The boundary between "Character" and "Reader" is intentionally blurred. The text addresses the reader as a Somanaut undergoing the same training as the protagonists.

### Phases of Engagement
1.  **Entry**: Orientation to the world's logic. (Book 1)
2.  **Cultivation**: Deepening the symbolic vocabulary. (Book 2)
3.  **Integration**: Connecting the fiction to the reader's reality. (Book 3)
4.  **Independence**: The reader transcends the need for the book. (The "Missing" Chapter / Afterword).
