## 1. HOUSE LUMINTH (The Weavers)
*Specialization: Aletheia Mapping (Narrative & Light)*

**Motto**: *"Lux Ex Tenebris"* (Light from Darkness)
**Khalorēē Mutation**: Enhanced visual cortex processing; ability to see "Narrative Threads" as literal light spectrums.

### The Lineage
*   **Grandmaster Zenith Luminth (2080-2165)**
    *   Founder of the **Aletheia Protocol**. Discovered that history behaves like a mutable text.
    *   *Status*: Deceased (Ascended).
*   **Dr. Nova Luminth (b. 2145)**
    *   Current Matriarch. Orthodox Weaver. Believes narratives should be stabilized, not rewritten.
    *   *Relation*: Corvan's Mother.
*   **Dr. Corvan "Corv" Luminth (b. 2192)**
    *   **The Renegade**. Believes the "Script" is a prison.
    *   *Role*: Lead Psycho-Pathologist, Somanaut Team 7.
*   **Aurora Luminth (b. 2194)**
    *   Corvan's sister. Experimental physicist.
    *   *Status*: Missing in the Latent Space.

### Family Tension
House Luminth is currently divided between the **Orthodox Weavers** (keep the story safe) and the **Radical Editors** (Corvan's faction: rewrite the ending).

## 2. HOUSE QUORIL (The Architects)
*Specialization: Resonance Topology (Math & Structure)*

**Motto**: *"Ordo Ab Chao"* (Order from Chaos)
**Khalorēē Mutation**: Synesthesia (Seeing sound as geometry). High-functioning structural logic.

### The Lineage
*   **Maestro Kaelen Quoril (2085-2170)**
    *   Composer-Mathematician. Created the **Resonance Engine** logic.
    *   *Status*: Deceased.
*   **Director Node Quoril (b. 2150)**
    *   CEO of Quoril Systems. Focuses on industrial application of resonance.
    *   *Relation*: Jian's Father.
*   **Madame Frequency Quoril (b. 2152)**
    *   Mystic. Focuses on the "Song of the Spheres."
    *   *Relation*: Jian's Mother.
*   **Dr. Jian Quoril (b. 2195)**
    *   **The Bridge**. Attempts to synthesize his father's logic with his mother's mysticism.
    *   *Role*: Lead Neuro-Cartographer, Somanaut Team 7.

### Family Tension
The "Quoril Schism": The industrial faction wants to patent and sell Resonance tech; the mystic faction believes it is sacred and should be free. Jian is caught in the middle.

## 3. HOUSE VIRETH (The Alchemists)
*Specialization: Metabolic Emergence (Biology & Emotion)*

**Motto**: *"Vita Mutatur"* (Life is Changed, Not Taken)
**Khalorēē Mutation**: Radical Empathy. Permeable blood-brain barrier allowing direct sensing of external neuro-chemistry.

### The Lineage
*   **Grandmother Amira Vireth (2100-2190)**
    *   The "Great Healer." Developed the **Soma-Sync** technique.
    *   *Status*: Deceased (Her memory is the "Note" vector for Sona).
*   **Dr. Hassan Vireth (b. 2140)**
    *   Military Bio-Engineer. Weaponized empathy for interrogation.
    *   *Relation*: Sona's Father (Estranged).
*   **Dr. Sona Vireth (b. 2198)**
    *   **The Pure Vessel**. Refuses to weaponize her gift.
    *   *Role*: Lead Bio-Acoustic Engineer, Somanaut Team 7.
*   **Ahmed Vireth (b. 2200)**
    *   Sona's brother. Joined the "Mercy Killers" (using empathy to euthanize the suffering).

### Family Tension
House Vireth struggles with the "Curse of Feeling." Half the family retreats into isolation to avoid pain; the other half (Hassan) tries to control it. Sona seeks a third way: Transmutation.

## 4. HOUSE SETER (The Guardians)
*Specialization: Tzimtzum Shielding (Time & Defense)*

**Motto**: *"Non Nobis"* (Not for Us)
**Khalorēē Mutation**: Hyper-dense biofield. Naturally resistant to entropy and time-dilation.

### The Lineage
*   **General Avon Seter (2110-2195)**
    *   Founder of the **Aegis Matrix**. Established the Guardian Corps.
    *   *Status*: Retired.
*   **Colonel Density Seter (b. 2155)**
    *   "The Iron Wall." Believes protection means absolute isolation.
    *   *Relation*: Gideon's Father.
*   **Gideon Seter (b. 2190)**
    *   **The Open Shield**. Believes true protection allows for vulnerability.
    *   *Role*: Lead Systems Immunologist, Somanaut Team 7.
*   **Sarah Seter (b. 2192)**
    *   Gideon's sister. Left the Corps to become a "Free Runner" (smuggling data through the Panopticon).

### Family Tension
The definition of "Safety." The Orthodox Seters believe in walls. Gideon believes in "permeable boundaries" (The Cell Membrane model vs. The Fortress model).
