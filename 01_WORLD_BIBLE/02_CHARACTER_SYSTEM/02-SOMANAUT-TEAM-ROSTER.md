# 02 - Somanaut Team Roster

*This document provides the core profiles for the Somanaut team of the `Anamnesis Engine` expedition. It details their roles, psychological drivers, and biological signatures within the `Somatic Canticles` universe.*

*Last Updated: 2026-01-25*

---

## 1. Dr. Jian Quoril - The Neuro-Cartographer (House Quoril)

- **Role:** Neuro-Cartographer
- **Specialty:** Somatic Datascape Mapping & Resonance Topology.
- **Dominant Neuro-Endocrine Axis (Enneagram):** **Pituitary-Thalamic Axis (Type 5 - The Investigator).** His system prioritizes higher-order cognitive processing, pattern recognition, and data synthesis. He perceives the universe as information requiring structure.
- **Function on Team:** Jian is the map-maker. He renders the chaotic, non-linear biological environment into traversable, three-dimensional maps (The Manas Interface). His mind is the high-command for the expedition, providing the team with their sense of location, direction, and structural understanding.
- **Psychological Profile:** A brilliant and incisive intellectual who finds immense security in knowledge and competence. He can be emotionally detached, viewing feelings as corrupted or unreliable data. His approach is defined by the "Ordo Ab Chao" motto of his House.
- **Narrative Arc:** From Rigid Logic to Integrated Wisdom. He must learn that the map is not the territory and that some truths can only be understood through direct, embodied experience.

---

## 2. Gideon Seter - The Systems Immunologist (House Seter)

- **Role:** Systems Immunologist
- **Specialty:** Bio-Threat Assessment & Tzimtzum Compression Shielding.
- **Dominant Neuro-Endocrine Axis (Enneagram):** **Adrenal Axis (Type 8 - The Challenger).** His physiology is primed for threat response. His baseline production of cortisol and adrenaline is higher than average, making him hyper-aware of danger but also giving him immense resilience (The Klei Toda'ah).
- **Function on Team:** Gideon is the shield. He is the team's anchor to the primal, reptilian reality of the body. He identifies and neutralizes biological threats. In the datascapes, his Khalorēē Field Architecture is a gravitational force that can stabilize the team during moments of high stress.
- **Psychological Profile:** Decisive, grounded, and fiercely protective. His worldview is often binary: threat/non-threat, safe/unsafe. He fears being controlled or overwhelmed above all else. He embodies the "Non Nobis" (Not for Us) service ethos of his House.
- **Narrative Arc:** From Protector to Guardian. He must learn that true strength allows for vulnerability, transitioning from building walls to holding space.

---

## 3. Dr. Sona Vireth - The Bio-Acoustic Engineer (House Vireth)

- **Role:** Bio-Acoustic Engineer
- **Specialty:** Metabolic Emergence & Emotional Frequency Analysis.
- **Dominant Neuro-Endocrine Axis (Enneagram):** **Cardiac-Thymic Axis (Type 4 - The Individualist).** Her system is uniquely attuned to the subtle, resonant frequencies of the body. She experiences the bio-field as a symphony (Adawat al-Wa'i).
- **Function on Team:** Sona is the sensor. She is the team's emotional and intuitive compass. She can distinguish the "frequency of fear" from the "frequency of joy" in the vibrations of a cell membrane. She hears the "music" beneath the biological noise.
- **Psychological Profile:** Deeply empathetic, intuitive, and creative. She feels everything, which is both her greatest gift and her greatest burden. She embodies the "Vita Mutatur" (Life is Changed, Not Taken) philosophy of the Alchemists.
- **Narrative Arc:** From Emotional Container to Resonant Channel. She must find the line between feeling a system's pain and being consumed by it, becoming a pure channel for health.

---

## 4. Dr. Corvan Luminth - The Psycho-Pathologist & Team Lead (House Luminth)

- **Role:** Psycho-Pathologist & Somanaut Team Lead
- **Specialty:** Aletheia Mapping & Narrative Diagnostics.
- **Dominant Neuro-Endocrine Axis (Enneagram):** **Integrated Tri-Axial Candidate (Type 9 - The Peacemaker).** His ultimate potential is to achieve a harmonious balance between the axes. He is the living embodiment of the Tryambakam Protocol in training.
- **Function on Team:** Corv is the storyteller. He is the central processor who synthesizes the logical maps, threat assessments, and emotional resonance into a single, coherent diagnosis (The Yìshí Qìxiè). He reads the *story* of the disease.
- **Psychological Profile:** A calm, patient, and holistic thinker who can see all sides of a problem. His deepest drive is to restore harmony ("Lux Ex Tenebris"). Inherited the "Weaver" mutation from his House.
- **Narrative Arc:** From Passive Witness to Active Alchemist. He must learn that true harmony sometimes requires him to instigate a crisis (Severance) rather than smoothing things over.
