## III. Trilogy Narrative Progression (Minor Arcana)

The story's structural evolution follows the progression from **Aces** to **Tens** across the three books.

### Book 1: Diagnosis (Foundation — Aces through Fours)
- **Aces**: Awakening of the Entropy Plague (Inverted potential) vs. Somanaut Call.
- **Twos (Dominion/Peace)**: Initial attempts to control the field.
- **Threes (Virtue/Works)**: Establishing the primary medical protocols.
- **Fours (Completion/Power)**: Setting the physical containment for the Anamnesis Engine.

### Book 2: Healing (Conflict & Integration — Fives through Sevens)
- **Fives (Strife/Worry)**: The "Healing Crisis"—confronting the redacted belief shadow.
- **Sixes (Victory/Success)**: Initial coherence achieved; successful re-binding of collective modules.
- **Sevens (Valour/Failure)**: The ultimate test of persistence against the "Ghost Frequencies" of previous aeons.

### Book 3: Liberation (Mastery — Eights through Tens)
- **Eights (Swiftness/Prudence)**: Rapid data transmission and preparation for the Severance.
- **Nines (Strength/Happiness)**: Achieving high-amplitude gamma coherence.
- **Tens (Ruin/Wealth)**: The **Severance Event**—the ruin of the old consciousness structure (Ten of Swords) to gain the wealth of species-wide sovereignty (Ten of Disks).
