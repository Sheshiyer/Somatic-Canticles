## 🔐 Security Protocols: No Spiritual Bypassing

To prevent "gaming" the system (copy-pasting answers without understanding):
1. **Interactive Validation**: The final access requires an "interview" with automated or community-led protocols that test actual comprehension of the 13 Lenses.
2. **Context-Dependent Ciphers**: Some clues are generated based on the reader's unique path through the vault, preventing simple answer-sharing.
3. **Morphic Field Check**: The "proof of work" is the reader's demonstrated coherence (Mastery of the Witness state).
