## Psychological & Psychoanalytic Foundations

### The Trauma of Birth & Individuation

**Author**: Otto Rank
- **Excerpts**:
    - *Fear of Life vs. Fear of Death*: Life-fear is fear of separation/individuation; Death-fear is fear of union/merger.
    - *Three Stages of Evolution*: Pre-sexual (Totemic), Sexual (Paternal), and Post-sexual (Integrated).
- **SC Application**: The "Severance Event" in Book 3 is a literalized version of Rank's birth trauma/separation architecture—a species-wide individuation crisis.

### Feminine Psychology & The Lunar Archetype

**Author**: Erich Neumann
- **Excerpts**:
    - *The Fear of the Feminine*: Psychological stages of women's development and the Moon/Matriarchal consciousness.
- **SC Application**: Grounding for the Anahata Galaxy's cultural focus on emotional intelligence and the "Lunar Masteries" of characters like Sona.
