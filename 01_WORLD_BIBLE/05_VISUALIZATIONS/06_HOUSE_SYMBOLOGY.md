# House Symbology & Heraldry
*Visual Identity of the Four Great Lineages*

*Last Updated: 2026-01-25*

---

## 1. House Luminth (The Weavers)
**"Lux Ex Tenebris"**

### The Crest
*   **Visual Structure**: A fractal prism suspended in a void.
*   **Iconography**: A central "All-Seeing Eye" rendered not as biology, but as a convergence of infinite silver threads.
*   **Color Palette**:
    *   **Primary**: Bioluminescent Silver (#EAEAEA)
    *   **Secondary**: Void Black (#000000)
    *   **Accent**: Prism White (Spectral gradients)
*   **Symbolism**: Represents the "Aletheia" (Truth) that is woven from the raw data of the universe. The threads suggest that reality is a textile that can be edited.

---

## 2. House Quoril (The Architects)
**"Ordo Ab Chao"**

### The Crest
*   **Visual Structure**: A perfect circle containing complex Cymatic patterns.
*   **Iconography**: Geometric sound-waves frozen in gold. The pattern is usually the "Sri Yantra" or a hyper-dimensional cube.
*   **Color Palette**:
    *   **Primary**: Resonance Gold (#D4AF37)
    *   **Secondary**: Royal Navy Blue (#000080)
    *   **Accent**: Electric Cyan (Data nodes)
*   **Symbolism**: Represents the mathematical underpinning of reality. The "Music of the Spheres" made visible. Order imposed on chaos through frequency.

---

## 3. House Vireth (The Alchemists)
**"Vita Mutatur"**

### The Crest
*   **Visual Structure**: An unfolding Fibonacci spiral that resembles both a leaf and a DNA helix.
*   **Iconography**: The "Green Lion" consuming the sun (alchemical symbol), re-interpreted as a cell dividing.
*   **Color Palette**:
    *   **Primary**: Deep Emerald (#50C878)
    *   **Secondary**: Blood Red (Subtle veins)
    *   **Accent**: Sunlight Gold
*   **Symbolism**: Represents the "Becoming." Life is not static; it is a constant process of transmutation. The spiral indicates evolution and eternal return.

---

## 4. House Seter (The Guardians)
**"Non Nobis"**

### The Crest
*   **Visual Structure**: A heavy, shield-like hexagon with a dense gravitational center.
*   **Iconography**: A "Black Hole" or "Singularity" surrounded by an impenetrable event horizon (The Shield).
*   **Color Palette**:
    *   **Primary**: Gunmetal Grey (#536872)
    *   **Secondary**: Gravity Blue (Almost black)
    *   **Accent**: Warning Orange (Minimal)
*   **Symbolism**: Represents "Tzimtzum" (Contraction). The ability to compress one's field into a point so dense that nothing can harm it. A fortress of solitude.
