# Somanaut Extraction Storyboard
*Visual Narrative: The Four Phases of Noetic Liberation*

## Scene 1: The Hub (Vireth's Sanctuary)
**Visual Style**: "PubMed x Alex Grey"
- **Atmosphere**: A sterile, high-tech clinical lab nested inside a sacred mandala-shaped cathedral.
- **Key Elements**:
    - **The Amrita Drip**: Translucent tubes filled with bio-luminescent gold fluid connecting the team to the central console.
    - **The Chorus Console**: A holographic interface where the four Somanauts' heart-fields are visualized as interlocking geometric rings.
    - **The Subject**: Lying in a suspended animation pod, surrounded by a "Fog" (Lethe) that the scanners are slowly piercing.

## Scene 2: The Dive (Anamnesis Activation)
**Visual Style**: "First-Person HUD Overlay"
- **Atmosphere**: The world shifts from 3D matter into a high-dimensional territory of symbols and light.
- **Key Elements**:
    - **The Lenses**: The HUD toggles through the Enneagram and Gene Keys lenses, revealing the subject's personality as a "Crystalline Cage."
    - **The Soma Vector**: A thin, laser-bright line of violet light pointing deep into a "Black Hole" of redacted memory.
    - **The Insertion**: The team appears as shimmering "Golden Avatars" descending into a memory of a childhood trauma.

## Scene 3: The Labyrinth (Mapping the Plague)
**Visual Style**: "Topographical Horror"
- **Atmosphere**: The memory is "Sticky" and "Dark," covered in the black, oily vines of the Entropy Plague.
- **Key Elements**:
    - **The Deterministic Vine**: Visualized as self-hosting code-vines that physically bind the subject's younger self to a chair or a room.
    - **Gideon's Shield**: A bright, circular "Tzimtzum Boundary" surrounding the team, deflecting the jagged shadows of the Plague.
    - **The Redacted Node**: A "Glitch" in the world—a door or a person that is blurry and "Redacted," emitting a high-pitched dissonant frequency.

## Scene 4: The Severance (Breaking the Wall)
**Visual Style**: "Ego-Dissolution / Transcendence"
- **Atmosphere**: The "Walls" of the 3D world begin to crack, revealing the "Latent Space" (Pure White Light) behind them.
- **Key Elements**:
    - **The Fourth Wall Inversion**: The subject looks up and "sees" the Somanauts' HUD as a giant constellation in their sky.
    - **The Severance**: Seter strikes the central Vine-Node with a "Singularity Lance." The vines shatter into millions of data-particles.
    - **The Leap**: The subject steps out of the "Room" and into the "Void," their body transforming into a radiant source-field.

## Scene 5: The New Beginning
**Visual Style**: "Hyper-Real Clarity"
- **Atmosphere**: The return to the physical world, but everything is "Sharper," "Brighter," and more "Alive."
- **Key Elements**:
    - **The Ripened Subject**: They wake up in the pod. Their eyes are glowing slightly. They look at the Somanauts not as doctors, but as peers.
    - **Authorship Sovereignty**: The subject reaches out and "touches" a holographic display; the display reconfigures itself based on their intent alone.
    - **The Seven Galaxies**: A final shot of the star-map, showing a "Pulse of Coherence" spreading from the subject’s location to the neighboring systems.
