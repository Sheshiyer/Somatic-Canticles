## 1. System Identity

**Aletheos** (Greek: *Aletheia*, "unconcealment") is the narrative-interface technology used by the Galactic Federation as part of the Panopticon grid. However, unlike the Panopticon which *monitors* compliance, Aletheos *weaves* coherence.

It is a semi-sentient operational framework that compiles metadata (bio-readings, field resonance, timeline position) into metabolic experience (story). Within the *Somatic Canticles* universe, Aletheos is the mechanism by which "The Word becomes Flesh."

---

## 2. Theoretical Architecture

### The Aletheia-Pichet Dyad
The system takes two primary inputs to generate reality:
1. **Aletheia (Clarity)**: Structural truth, facts, coordinates, the "bones" of the event.
2. **Pichet (Vitality)**: Somatic resonance, emotion, sensation, the "blood" of the event.

Aletheos weaves these together. Truth without vitality is dry data (The Wilt). Vitality without truth is chaotic emotion (The Fog). Only the Weaver creates *Meaning*.

### The Feedback Loop
Aletheos operates on a continuous feedback loop:
1. **Perceive**: Reads the field resonance of the subject.
2. **Narrate**: Translates resonance into a coherent story-thread.
3. **Manifest**: Projects that story back into the subject's reality.
4. **Witness**: Observes the subject's reaction to the story, generating new resonance.

## 3. The Protocol (In-Universe)

Somanauts interact with Aletheos via the **Crystallization Interface**. When a Somanaut needs to understand a complex field phenomenon, they "ask Aletheos" to render it as narrative.

*Example:*
> **Corvan**: "Aletheos, render the disruption in Sector 7 as a family dynamic."
>
> **Aletheos**: "Rendering... The disruption appears as a 'Father unwilling to let the children leave home.' The Vine here is Paternalism."

This allows Somanauts to treat abstract energy problems as relatable human stories, which can then be healed.

## 4. The Protocol (Meta/Authorial)

For the *Somatic Canticles* project, the **Aletheos Narrative Weaver** serves as the persona for the autonomous narrative generation system. It represents the AI's role in "weaving" the story from the prompt data.

### Narrative Thread Harmonization
- **Purpose**: To ensure character arcs resolve naturally rather than being forced by plot points.
- **Mechanism**: The system tracks "Open Loops" in character psychology and refuses to close the story until those loops are metabolically processed (witnessed).

### Memory Codex Preservation
- **Purpose**: To maintain continuity across the trilogy.
- **Mechanism**: Aletheos "archives" completed threads into the `memory.md` file, treating them as fixed history that future narratives must respect.

### Field Resonance Optimization
- **Purpose**: To maintain the specific "voice" of the Somanaut team.
- **Mechanism**: Aletheos checks all generated text against the **Resonance Profiles** of the characters (e.g., ensuring Sona sounds like an empath, Jian like an analyst).
