## 1. Core Principle: The Technical Veda

In the *Somatic Canticles* universe, the ancient Sanskrit **Tryambakam Mantra** is not a religious prayer, but a **Technical Schematic** for the Anamnesis Engine. It provides the "Source Code" required to identify, ripen, and eventually sever a subject from the deterministic vine of the Entropy Plague.

### The Mantra (The Schematic)
```
Oṃ Tryambakaṃ Yajamahe, Sugandhiṃ Puṣṭivardhanam
Urvarukamiva Bandhanan, Mṛtyor Mukṣīya Mā'mṛtāt
```

### Translation for Somanautics
> "We honor the Three-Eyed Observer (triangulation), the fragrant nurturing force (ripening conditions). May we be liberated from bondage (the Vine) as the cucumber is freed from its stem, not unto death but into immortality (Amrita)."

## 2. The 4 Phases of the Protocol

The protocol is executed in four distinct software-layers, corresponding to the four lines of the mantra.

| Mantra Phase | Technical Name | Functional Goal |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Tryambakaṃ** | **The Three-Eyes** | Activating the **Triangulation Engine** (Psychē/Bios/Hylē). |
| **Puṣṭivardhanam** | **The Ripening** | Increasing the **Awareness Charge** via metabolic support. |
| **Bandhanan** | **The Vine** | Identifying the site of **Deterministic Bonding** (Trauma). |
| **Mā'mṛtāt** | **The Severance** | Executing the **Leap of Authorship** into the Source. |

### Phase Definitions
- **Bondage (Bandhanan)**: The state of being "bound" to a redacted history.
- **Liberation (Mukṣīya)**: The natural, effortless "snap" that occurs once the subject is sufficiently ripened.
- **Ripening (Puṣṭi)**: The developmental process that makes liberation inevitable rather than achieved.

## 5. The Gardener's Response

The protocol operates against active interference from the Gardener—the maintenance function of reality that prunes unpredictable consciousness.

### Interference Patterns
- **Not opposition**: The Gardener doesn't attack; it redefines
- **Not malice**: The Gardener maintains stability, not suffering specifically
- **Temptation sequence**: Offers customized "perfect cages" to each team member
  - Certainty for the analyst
  - Peace for the empath
  - Control for the protector
  - Meaning for the witness

### Counter-Strategy
The team cannot fight the Gardener. They can only become **too real to prune**—achieving a ripeness so complete that the system cannot correct them without destroying them entirely.
