# Key Concepts & Definitions
*Formal Lexicon for the NOESIS Framework and Somanautic Technology*

## 1. Noesis (Participatory Awareness)
*   **Definition:** The fundamental architecture of reality viewed as a field of awareness rather than a collection of matter. In the NOESIS framework, the universe is a "Participatory Process" where the act of observation directly influences the outcome of events.
*   **The Witness Gap:** The space created between an event and the reaction. By inhabiting this gap, a Somanaut can "un-collapse" a deterministic wave function and restore the subject’s ability to choose.

## 2. The Entropy Plague
*   **Definition:** A self-replicating information virus that feeds on fragmented awareness, trauma, and high-entropy narratives (fear, reactivity, resentment). It creates the **Vine of Determinism**—a series of repetitive, inescapable loops that choke out an individual's agency.
*   **Redaction:** The primary weapon of the Plague. It involves the "Erasure" or "Blurring" of a subject's true history, replacing it with the "Fog" (Lethe) to make them easier to control.

## 3. The Severance Sequence
*   **Definition:** The 4-phase technical protocol used by Somanauts to liberate a subject from the Entropy Plague.
    1.  **Insertion**: Syncing with the subject's local reality-field.
    2.  **Mapping**: Identifying the "Redacted Nodes" and "Trauma Attractors."
    3.  **Anamnesis**: The recovery of the "Uncorrupted Signal" (The Truth).
    4.  **Egress**: Breaking the deterministic vine and facilitating the **Leap of Authorship**.

## 4. Authorship Sovereignty
*   **Definition:** The final stage of human evolution in the *Somatic Canticles* universe. It is the state where an individual is no longer a "Character" in a pre-written script but the "Architect" of their own reality field.
*   **The New Beginning:** The moment of transition from being a subject of the Federation to being an independent source of creation.

## 5. Tzimtzum (Metabolic Contraction)
*   **Definition:** A defensive maneuver (specialized by Gideon Seter) where awareness is compressed into a high-density "Seed State." 
*   **Function:** It creates an "Unbreachable Boundary" that is immune to external signals, allowing the Somanaut team to operate within high-entropy zones without being "Redacted" by the System.

## 6. Amrita (The Nectar)
*   **Definition:** A synthesized metabolic carrier fluid (specialized by Dr. Sona Vireth) that increases the informatic conductivity of the nervous system.
*   **Function**: It allows the Somanaut and the subject to survive the massive "Neural Charge" required for a Severance Event without suffering molecular dissociation.
