# The Severance Sequence
*The Extraction Lifecycle: The Four Phases of Noetic Liberation*

## 1. Core Principle: The Extraction Lifecycle

In the *Somatic Canticles* universe, an **Extraction** (The Severance) is a high-risk medical and narrative procedure. It is the act of physically and psychologically "Severing" a subject from a corrupted, deterministic history and replanting them into a "Ripened" future. This process follows a strict 4-phase protocol known as the **Severance Sequence**.

### The Transformation Curve
The sequence is designed to manage the "Awareness Charge" of the subject, ensuring they don't "Burn Out" or "Scatter" during the move.

| Phase | Technical Name | Team Action | Subject State |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I** | **Insertion** | Syncing/Anchoring | The Fog (Lethe) |
| **II** | **Mapping** | Identifying Nodes | Awareness (Noesis)|
| **III** | **Anamnesis** | Restoring Truth | Insight (Recovery) |
| **IV** | **Egress** | Breaking the Vine| Freedom (Sovereignty)|

## 2. Phase-by-Phase Breakdown

### Phase I: Insertion (The Dive)
*Synchronizing with the Baseline*
- **Team**: Seter (Shielding), Vireth (Bio-Sync).
- **Goal**: To match the "Frequency" of the subject's local reality-field without alerting the Panopticon.
- **Fail-Condition**: "Signal Rejection"—the subject's immune system or the Entropy Plague repels the team.

### Phase II: Mapping (The Search)
*Identifying the Redacted Nodes*
- **Team**: Luminth (Topology), Quoril (Diagnostics).
- **Goal**: To find the "Root Cause" of the subject's deterministic trap. This is the "Debugging" of the history.
- **Fail-Condition**: "The Labyrinth"—the team getting lost in the subject's false memories or "Redacted Zones."

### Phase III: Anamnesis (The Recovery)
*Restoring the True Story*
- **Team**: The Chorus (Collective Witness).
- **Goal**: The most dangerous part. The team collectively "Witnesses" the true event, forcing the Entropy Plague to "Let go" of the memory.
- **Fail-Condition**: "Metabolic Collapse"—the subject's brain cannot handle the "True Signal," leading to a fatal crash.

### Phase IV: Egress (The Severance)
*Breaking the Vine*
- **Team**: Seter (Anchor), Luminth (Pathway).
- **Goal**: Cutting the remaining connections to the "Old World" and guiding the subject into the "New Beginning."
- **Fail-Condition**: "Incomplete Severance"—the subject "snaps back" into their old life, often with severe traumatic amnesia.

## 3. Post-Sequence: The Re-Base

After the sequence is complete, the subject enters a period of **Integrative Recovery**.

- **Narration Rehabilitation**: The subject is taught how to "Write" their new life, ensuring the "Entropy Plague" cannot find a hollow space to re-infect.
- **Biometric Stabilization**: Tapering off the Amrita support and ensuring the subject's biological "Three Bodies" are in a state of stable homeostatic flow.
