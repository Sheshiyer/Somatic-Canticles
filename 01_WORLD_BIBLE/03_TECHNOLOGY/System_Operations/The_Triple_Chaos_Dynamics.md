## 1. Core Principle: Thalātha Fawḍā (Triple Chaos)

In the NOESIS framework, **Triple Chaos** is the realization that reality is fundamentally unpredictable and high-entropy until it is "Witnessed." The universe is composed of three interlocking "Bodies of Chaos"—**Psychē (Soul)**, **Bios (Body)**, and **Hylē (Matter)**. A Somanaut intervention is an act of "Ordered Observation" that temporarily domesticates this chaos to allow for a specific recovery of truth.

## 2. Strange Attractors: The Centers of Becoming

Within the turbulence of Triple Chaos, certain patterns tend to repeat. These are called **Strange Attractors**—the "Gravitational Sinks" where events coalesce.

- **The Trauma Attractor**: A "Whirlpool" of recurring pain (The Vine). It keeps the subject trapped in a specific historical script.
- **The Ripening Attractor**: The goal of the extraction—a new, stable basin of "Authorship" where the subject’s future is no longer governed by the chaos of the past.
