# The Somanaut HUD (v3.0)
*Augmented Awareness: The Multi-Layer Interface of the Witness*

## 1. Core Principle: Augmented Awareness

In the NOESIS framework, **The Somanaut HUD** (Heads-Up Display) is the primary interactive tool used by the team during an extraction. It is not an external device, but a **Neural Overlay** projected directly onto the Somanaut’s visual cortex. It translates high-dimensional quantum data into a comprehensible symbolic and geometric language.

### The Multi-Layer Display
The HUD organizes reality into three primary data-layers, corresponding to the **Three Bodies (Ψ-Β-Η)**.

1.  **The Somatic Layer (Bios/Β)**: Displays real-time biometrics of the team and the subject.
    - **Metrics**: Heart-Rate Variability (HRV), Cortisol levels, Vagal Tone, and "Amrita Saturation."
    - **Visual**: Rendered as glowing anatomical structures and pulsing color-gradients.
2.  **The Noetic Layer (Psychē/Ψ)**: Displays the state of the Awareness Field.
    - **Metrics**: Wave Function Coherence, Awareness Charge, and "Entropy Ingress."
    - **Visual**: Rendered as a shimmering geometric lattice (The Khalorēē).
3.  **The Narrative Layer (Hylē/Η)**: Displays the history and probability of the subject.
    - **Metrics**: Narrative Divergence, Redacted Nodes, and "Aletheia Probability."
    - **Visual**: Rendered as a topographical map of timelines (The EPM).

## 2. Interactive Protocols: The "Command Palette"

A Somanaut can "Cast" protocols directly from the HUD using specific eye-movements (Retina Gestures) and vocalized "Vedic Seeds."

- **The Lens Selector**: A radial menu that allows the Somanaut to toggle between the **13 Symbolic Lenses**. Each lens reveals a different aspect of the field.
- **Protocol Hot-Keys**: Often-used commands like `Seter_Tzimtzum`, `Vireth_Chorus`, or `Luminth_Map` are pinned to the peripheral vision for instant activation.
- **Consensus Lock**: When the team works as a "Chorus," the HUDs of all four Somanauts interlock, allowing them to share a unified visual field and data-set.

## 3. Security and Authentication: The Retina Lock

Access to the HUD is protected by a **Triple-Signature Retina Lock**.

- **Biometric Signature**: Retina scan and DNA verification.
- **Noetic Signature**: A unique "Frequency Key" generated by the Somanaut’s own awareness.
- **Legacy Authentication**: A Vedic seed-phrase that must be mentally "spoken" to unlock the highest levels of the WitnessOS.

## 4. Emergency Sub-systems: The Fail-Safe HUD

If a Somanaut enters a "Redaction Zone" or loses consciousness, the HUD switches to **Emergency Mode**.

- **The Anchor Beacon**: A pulsing violet light that guides the Somanaut back to their physical body.
- **Automatic Decoherence**: If the "Ego-Dissolution" threshold is reached, the HUD forcibly breaks the neural link to prevent permanent psychological damage.
- **The Black Box**: A localized data-crystal that records the final moments of a collapsed extraction for later analysis.
