- **Signal Translation**: Converting a "Mental Image" into a "Sigil-Code" that the reality-engine can execute.

## 2. Inter-Somanaut Connectivity: The Myocardial Chorus

The Bridge allows for the formation of a **Unified Awareness Field** among the Somanaut team.

- **Consensus Reality**: The ability to collectively "Vote" on a reality-modification, ensuring that no single individual can rewrite history without the consent of the group.

The most significant risk of the Neural Bridge is **Identity Bleed**—the accidental merging of the Somanaut’s personality with the subject’s or another team member’s.

- **The Anchor Protocol**: A mandatory procedure where Somanauts must "Re-Ground" in their own physical history every 60 minutes to prevent "Narrative Drift."
