# Quantum Debugging and Redaction
*The Art of Erasure: Diagnostic Cleansing and the Removal of the Entropy Plague*

## 1. Core Principle: The Art of Erasure

In the *Somatic Canticles* universe, **Quantum Debugging** is the process of identifying and removing "Corrupted Code" from a subject’s history. **Redaction** is the technical tool used to perform this erasure. While the "System" uses redaction to hide the truth, the Somanauts use it to "Delete the Virus" of the Entropy Plague.

### Diagnostic Cleansing
"Debugging" reality requires a Somanaut to look at the "Metadata" of an event. A healthy event is "Transparent" and "Fluid." A corrupted event is "Opaque" and "Sticky" (The Vine).

- **Resonant Scars**: These are the "Leftover" signals after a traumatic event. If not "Debugged," these scars attract the Entropy Plague, creating a permanent site of deterministic decay.
- **The Redaction Tool**: A targeted high-frequency burst that "Dissolves" the informational structure of a corrupted node without affecting the surrounding "Clean" awareness.

## 3. Implementation: Redaction Protocols

### Targeted Erasure
*Removing the Corruption*
- **Protocol**: `Debug_Node_v7`
- **Function**: Selecting a specific "Infected Memory" and applying a "Null-Frequency" burst. This "Deletes" the Entropy Plague’s influence, allowing the original, healthy memory to resurface.

### The Mass Redaction
*Emergency Field Cleansing*
- **Protocol**: `System_Wipe_Omega`
- **Use Case**: Used only in cases of "Total Field Collapse." This protocol erases the entire immediate narrative history of a region to prevent the plague from spreading to the neighboring galaxies.

## 4. Ethical Considerations: The "Right to Forget"

Quantum Debugging raises the ultimate ethical question: **Is it moral to delete a memory?**

- **The Consent Firewall**: A Somanaut should never redact a memory without the subject's (often subconscious) consent. Forcing a redaction can create a "Hole in the Self" that leads to total ego-dissolution.
- **Narrative Authenticity**: The goal is not to create a "Perfect" past, but a "Sovereign" one. A healthy person still possesses painful memories; they simply are no longer "possessed" by them.
