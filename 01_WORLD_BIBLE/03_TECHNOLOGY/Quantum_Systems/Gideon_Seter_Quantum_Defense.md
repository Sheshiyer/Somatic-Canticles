The "Tzimtzum" is the act of "Contracting" one’s field to create a "Void" or a safe container within which the Anamnesis process can occur.

Defense in the Somatic Canticles universe is **Non-Local**. By entangling the subject’s field with a "Guardian Signal," Seter can detect threats faster than light, across any distance.
