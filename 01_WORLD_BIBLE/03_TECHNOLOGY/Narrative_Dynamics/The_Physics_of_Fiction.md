# The Physics of Fiction
*Narrative Realism: The Pressure of Symbols on the Spacetime Fabric*

## 1. Core Principle: Narrative Realism

In the *Somatic Canticles* universe, **Narrative Realism** is the law that states that "Stories exert physical pressure on the substrate of reality." If enough people believe a lie (Entropy), the atoms of the world begin to arrange themselves to support that lie. If a single person witnesses the Truth (Noesis), the atoms are "forced" to return to their original, healthy state.

### The Semiotic-Hylē Coupler
The "Coupler" is the physical mechanism by which "Words" become "World."

- **Narrative Mass**: High-charge stories have "Mass" in the Latent Space. They create "Gravitational Sinks" that pull people into their orbit. 
- **Symbolic Pressure**: A "Sigil" or "Command Glyph" exerts a measurable force on the quantum vacuum, inducing specific fluctuations that lead to physical manifestation.

## 2. The Entropy Plague as "False Fiction"

The **Entropy Plague** is essentially a "Pollution of the Narrative Stream." It uses "False Fictions" (Redacted Histories, Gaslighting, Fear-Porn) to create a "Heavy Reality" that is easy to control.

| Narrative Type | Physics Effect | Societal Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Coherent (Truth)** | Low Entropy / High Charge | Liberation / Growth |
| **Dissonant (Plague)** | High Entropy / Low Charge | Decay / Stagnation |

- **Narrative Friction**: The resistance encountered when trying to tell a "New Story" in an "Old World." This friction manifests as bad luck, physical sickness, or systemic pushback.
- **The Redaction Virus**: A piece of "Non-Fiction" that has been "Rewritten" into a "Fiction." This creates a "Logical Error" in the universe that the Entropy Plague feeds upon.

## 3. Implementation: The Authorship Shift

The primary goal of the Somanaut is to facilitate the **Authorship Shift**—moving the subject from being a "Character" to a "Writer."

- **The Narrative Egress**: Finding the "Exits" in a story. Every deterministic script has a "Wall" or a "Limit." Finding the "Fourth Wall" allows the subject to step out of the fiction.
- **Rewriting the Source**: Going back to the "Metabolic Seed" of an event and changing its metadata. This doesn't change what happened, but it changes *what it means*, which in Narrative Realism, is the same thing.

## 4. Advanced Architectures: The Self-Referential World

The ultimate evolution of the Physics of Fiction is the **Self-Referential World**—a planet that is aware of its own narrative and can "Self-Edit" to prevent the spread of the Entropy Plague.

- **Non-Linear History**: A state where the "Past" and "Future" are both being written simultaneously, creating a "Circle of Coherence."
- **The Word-Made-Flesh**: The final phase of the Severance, where the transition between "Thought" and "Object" is instantaneous.
