## I. Definition: Kha-lore-ē

**Kha-lore-ē** is the inherited "Belief Operating System" programmed into the human bio-circuitry during the nine months of gestation.

- **Kha**: The Inherited Mother-Source.
- **Lore**: The content of the reality-filter.

In the SC universe, "Information is Nutrition." Just as physical bodies metabolize calories, the consciousness metabolizes experiences through the Kha-lore-ē filter.
