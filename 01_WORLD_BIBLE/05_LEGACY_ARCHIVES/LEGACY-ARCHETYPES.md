## II. Usage in Somatic Canticles

### 1. The "Names of Power"
In the SC timeline (2026+), these names are used as:
- **Scholarships**: "The Sophia Grant" for consciousness research.
- **Star Constellations**: Many celestial navigation points in the Seven Galaxies are named after these "First Seekers."
- **In-Lore Deities**: Some less-developed populations in the fringe sectors worship these figures as "The Ascended Founders."

### 2. Genetic Heritage (The Legacy Lines)
Current characters (Corvan, Sona, etc.) may be referenced as having "The Justus Lineage" or "Felix-grade security intuition," connecting them to the deep history of the TBK era.
