# Strategic Plan Snapshot: Somatic Canticles `v0.2`

> Active execution tracking lives in `tasks/todo.md`.
> This file is the high-level sequencing snapshot, not the live checklist.

**Goal:** Preserve the finished compiled trilogy package while keeping the intake, mapping, and authority work legible as a supporting research layer for any later revision, edition, or release push.

## Operating Constraints

- The active canonical manuscript count is `27` chapters.
- The `22`-asset arcana layer is a symbolic and visual taxonomy, not a chapter-count contract.
- `06_WORKBENCH/SC_STORYOPS/` is the staging lane for trilogy-wide intake, mapping, and downstream packet preparation.
- The project should not be described as fully release-ready while source and image mapping remain partial.

## Phase 1: Authority and Canon Lock
- Maintain canonical chapter count and chapter naming
- Maintain arcana-vs-chapter policy
- Keep `SC_STORYOPS` sequencing and authority language consistent with the finished compiled package
- Continue doc-drift cleanup where copy still contradicts the current package state

## Phase 2: Supporting Research Layer
- Keep vault-domain, concept, lore, and image maps available as supporting authority surfaces
- Continue external-handle representation where a future revision needs unavailable corpus support
- Treat unfinished mapping as research debt, not as a blocker to the current compiled manuscript package

## Phase 3: Future Revision Control
- Use `story/intake/book_projection_board.md` and related StoryOps surfaces to justify any future concept-to-book or chapter-level revision
- Keep future late-book edits tied to packet, delta-ledger, and authority surfaces rather than intuition
- Avoid widening doctrine, macro-governance, or symbolism beyond the current support stack

## Phase 4: Manuscript Maintenance
- Compare any future staged chapter work against current `02_MANUSCRIPTS/COMPILED/` text before merge-back
- Keep dialogue, terminology, and doctrine aligned to the editorial authority stack
- Keep omnibus, bibliography, glossary, and closing surfaces synchronized with compiled chapter changes

## Phase 5: Export and Release Readiness
- Maintain `canon/export ready` compiled surfaces
- Decide separately whether `research/image mapping incomplete` needs to be closed for a future edition or release cycle
- Re-evaluate broader release-readiness claims in the context of publication, packaging, and marketing rather than manuscript completion alone

## Current Proof Condition

The current manuscript cycle is complete. The remaining proof condition is narrower: if the trilogy is revised again, the workbench must still be able to defensibly explain how editorial doctrine, world-bible canon, mounted research inputs, and image principles map into those revisions without forcing unsupported claims.
