# Strategic Plan Snapshot: Somatic Canticles `v0.2`

> Active execution tracking lives in `tasks/todo.md`.
> This file is the high-level sequencing snapshot, not the live checklist.

**Goal:** Preserve the trilogy's canonical/export-ready text while completing the intake, mapping, and authority work needed before any later production or release push.

## Operating Constraints

- The active canonical manuscript count is `27` chapters.
- The `22`-asset arcana layer is a symbolic and visual taxonomy, not a chapter-count contract.
- `06_WORKBENCH/SC_STORYOPS/` is the staging lane for trilogy-wide intake, mapping, and downstream packet preparation.
- The project should not be described as fully release-ready while source and image mapping remain partial.

## Phase 1: Clarify and Authority Lock
- Confirm canonical chapter count and chapter naming
- Confirm arcana-vs-chapter policy
- Confirm readiness language and current sequencing for `SC_STORYOPS`
- Continue downstream doc-drift cleanup where copy still contradicts the above

## Phase 2: Trilogy Intake Mapping
- Complete usable vault-domain coverage in `story/intake/vault_source_domains.md`
- Continue populating `story/intake/concept_registry.md`
- Continue stabilizing `story/intake/lore_worldbuilding_map.md`
- Continue building image retrieval semantics in `story/image_index.md` and `story/intake/image_principles.md`
- Keep external or unavailable inputs explicitly represented through handle registries rather than implicit memory

## Phase 3: Projection Before Assignment
- Use `story/intake/book_projection_board.md` to justify concept-to-book placement
- Keep per-book chapter lanes provisional until intake evidence is strong enough
- Avoid expanding packet work just because a thematic fit seems plausible

## Phase 4: Downstream Chapter Work
- Only deepen chapter packets after intake, lore, and image support converge
- Compare any staged chapter work against current `02_MANUSCRIPTS/COMPILED/` text before merge-back
- Keep dialogue, terminology, and doctrine aligned to the editorial authority stack

## Phase 5: Export and Release Readiness
- Maintain `canon/export ready` compiled surfaces
- Close the `research/image mapping incomplete` gap
- Re-evaluate broader release-readiness claims only after mapping is complete
- Treat deployment, packaging, and marketing work as downstream of the mapping proof condition

## Current Proof Condition

The current cycle is complete only when the workbench can defensibly explain how editorial doctrine, world-bible canon, mounted research inputs, and image principles map into the trilogy without forcing premature book or chapter assignments.
