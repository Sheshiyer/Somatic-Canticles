**THE VINE. THE WILT. THE WITNESS.**

**"The Matrix meets The Body Keeps the Score in this visionary trilogy of consciousness, connection, and the future of human evolution."**

In a world where "connection" is a subscription service and human intimacy is mediated by algorithms, a new technology promises the ultimate escape: **The Anamnesis Engine**. It was supposed to be the perfect immersive simulation. Instead, it woke something up.

**BOOK I: THE ANAMNESIS ENGINE**
Dr. Aris Thorne, a disgraced neuro-architect, discovers a ghost in the machine—a signal that isn't code, but *pulse*. As he races to identify the anomaly, he uncovers a terrifying truth: the simulation isn't crashing. It's *birthing*.

**BOOK II: THE MYOCARDIAL CHORUS**
The anomaly spreads. Across the globe, strangers begin to dream the same dream, feel the same heartbeat, and speak with a shared voice. They are the **Somanauts**—explorers of inner space. But as the "Myocardial Chorus" grows louder, the old world's immune system activates. The **Wilt** begins—a psychic entropy threatening to erase human individuality forever.

**BOOK III: THE RIPENING**
The final confrontation is not a war of weapons, but a battle for the soul of the species. As the Wilt consumes the planet's digital infrastructure, the Somanauts must initiate the **Severance**—a radical procedure to unplug humanity from its synthetic illusions and reconnect it to the living web of life.

**Somatic Canticles** is more than a sci-fi epic. It is a transmission. A map for the lost. A song for the severed. It asks the single most dangerous question of our time:

*What happens when we finally stop scrolling and start feeling?*
