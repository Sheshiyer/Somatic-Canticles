# Visual Identity Guidelines

## Visual Mood: Clinical Mysticism — Rigorous Sacred

The visual language bridges two opposing aesthetics: **clinical precision** (clean, structural, technical) and **visionary mysticism** (cosmic, organic, luminous). Think: anatomical diagrams meet Alex Grey's sacred anatomy paintings. Medical journals meet Tarot imagery.

### Core Principles

1. **Form follows biology:** Shapes derived from actual biological structures (neurons, hearts, sacred geometry)
2. **Light as consciousness:** Luminosity represents Aletheia (unconcealment); darkness represents Lethe (concealment)
3. **Precision + poetry:** Clean technical execution with cosmic/mystical overtones
4. **Embodied abstraction:** Abstract enough to feel visionary, specific enough to reference real biology

### Reference Aesthetics
- Alex Grey's "Anatomy of Consciousness" series (sacred biological art)
- Medical/anatomical illustration (Gray's Anatomy, PubMed journal imagery)
- Tarot Major Arcana (Rider-Waite-Smith deck aesthetics)
- Hard SF book covers (Tor Books literary SF design)
- Neuro-imaging visualizations (fMRI scans, neural network renderings)

### Avoid
- Generic new-age aesthetics (chakra clipart, vague energy mandalas)
- Purely clinical/sterile medical design (no warmth or mysticism)
- Fantasy illustration (dragons, swords, generic magic)
- Pulp SF aesthetics (spaceships, aliens, laser guns)

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

#### Deep Neural Indigo
**Hex:** `#1A1F3A`
**RGB:** 26, 31, 58

**Usage:** Primary brand color, backgrounds, headers, main text

**Psychology:** Depth, consciousness, mystery, the "dark matter" of the unconscious. Evokes both deep space and deep inner space. Grounding, contemplative, serious.

**Biological Reference:** The color of deep tissue, the limbic system, the mystery of the brain's interior

---

#### Soma Gold
**Hex:** `#D4AF37`
**RGB:** 212, 175, 55

**Usage:** Accents, highlights, call-to-action elements, chapter headings

**Psychology:** Illumination, the Soma Vector (pure signal), Aletheia (unconcealment). Warm, precious, elevated. Sacred wisdom.

**Biological Reference:** The golden light of healthy neurons firing, bioluminescence, the "glow" of coherence

## Typography

### Header Font: Avenir Next

**Weights:** Medium (500), Bold (700), Heavy (900)

**Usage:** Book titles, chapter headings, major headings, campaign headlines

**Rationale:** Clean geometric sans-serif with technical precision but humanist warmth. Modern without being cold. Pairs clinical with accessible.

**Fallback:** Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif

---

### Body Font: Charter

**Weights:** Regular (400), Bold (700)

**Usage:** Body copy, product descriptions, long-form content, email text

**Rationale:** Highly readable serif designed for screens. Scholarly but warm. Evokes literary SF without being pretentious. Excellent for extended reading.

**Fallback:** Georgia, Times New Roman, serif

---

### Accent Font: Input Mono

**Weights:** Regular (400)

**Usage:** Technical terminology, biological terms in dialogue, code-like elements, chapter numbers, Tarot card numbers (0-XXI)

**Rationale:** Monospace font signals "technical precision". Used sparingly for biological terminology ("cytokine storm", "vagal tone") to emphasize clinical accuracy.

**Fallback:** Courier New, monospace

---

### Typographic Hierarchy

| Element | Font | Size | Color | Style |
|---------|------|------|-------|-------|

## Logo Guidelines

### Logo Concept

**The Tryambakam Glyph** — three interlocking vectors forming a triangle (Triangulation Engine symbol):
- **Soma Vector** (vertical, gold)
- **Manas Interface** (horizontal left, indigo)
- **Muladhara Terminus** (horizontal right, crimson)

### Logo Variations

**Primary Lockup:** Full glyph + wordmark "SOMATIC CANTICLES" in Avenir Next Medium

**Icon Only:** Tryambakam Glyph alone (for social media avatars, favicons)

**Wordmark Only:** Text-only "SOMATIC CANTICLES" (for editorial use)

**Stacked Version:** Glyph above wordmark (for vertical spaces)

---

### Clear Space & Sizing

**Clear Space:** Minimum clear space = height of glyph on all sides. No other elements within this boundary.

**Minimum Size:**
- **Print:** 0.5 inches width for legibility
- **Digital:** 32px height minimum for icon-only glyph

---

### Color Usage

**Primary:** Soma Gold glyph + Deep Neural Indigo wordmark on Neuronal White background

**Reverse:** Soma Gold glyph + Neuronal White wordmark on Deep Neural Indigo background

**Monochrome:** Single color (any brand color) when full-color not available

---

### Placement Rules

- Always place in top-left or center for web/print layouts
- **Book spines:** vertical stacked version
- **Book covers:** primary lockup in top third or bottom third, never dead center
- **Marketing materials:** top-left corner within clear space

---

### Logo Misuse — Do NOT

- Rotate, skew, or distort glyph
- Change glyph proportions or vector angles
- Add drop shadows, gradients, or effects
- Place on busy backgrounds without clear space buffer

## Imagery & Art Direction

### Photography Style

**Mood:** Clinical + Cosmic

**Characteristics:**
- High contrast with deep blacks and luminous highlights
- Macro/close-up biological textures (neurons, heart tissue, cellular structures)
- Ethereal lighting (backlighting, rim lighting, bioluminescence effects)
- Minimal compositions with negative space
- Scientific/medical photography aesthetic meets visionary art

**Subject Matter:**
- Anatomical details (hands, hearts, neural pathways)
- Meditation/embodiment practices (close-ups of breath, posture)
- Abstract biological textures (cellular structures, tissue cross-sections)
- Tarot-inspired symbolic imagery (The Fool's leap, The Tower's upheaval)

**Avoid:**
- Generic stock photos of people meditating
- Cliché mystical imagery (crystals, incense, yoga mats)
- Literal SF imagery (spaceships, aliens)
- Overly sanitized or sterile medical photos

---

### Illustration Style

**Primary Style:** Sacred Anatomy Illustration

**Description:** Line art combining anatomical precision (medical illustration) with sacred geometry overlays. Think: Gray's Anatomy diagrams merged with Alex Grey's visionary anatomy paintings.

**Execution:**
- Fine line work (1-2px strokes) in Deep Neural Indigo
- Soma Gold accents for "lit" or "active" biological structures
- Geometric patterns (triangles, hexagons, Flower of Life) integrated into biological forms
- Transparent overlays showing multiple systems simultaneously (neural + cardiac + energetic)

**Use Cases:**
- Chapter opening illustrations (each Tarot card = biological event)
- Diagrams of Tryambakam Protocol, Triangulation Engine, Khalorēē Field Architecture
- Book cover central imagery
- Social media content (Instagram posts showing biological-mystical fusion)

## Graphic Elements

### Primary Shape: The Triangle (Triangulation Engine)

**Usage:** Primary geometric motif throughout all brand materials

**Variations:**
- Equilateral triangle pointing up (synthesis, integration)
- Three interlocking triangles (Tryambakam Glyph logo)
- Triangle grids/tessellations (background patterns)

**Meaning:** The Triangulation Engine (Soma Vector + Manas Interface + Muladhara Terminus). Three-eyed seeing. Tryambakam.

---

### Secondary Shapes

**Hexagon:** Sacred geometry, DNA structure, cellular organization

**Circle:** Wholeness, The World card (Tarot XXI), completion, homeostasis

**Spiral:** Growth, evolution, the unfolding of consciousness

---

### Textures

**Neural Network Pattern:** Fine web-like lines resembling synaptic connections. Subtle background texture.

**Cardiac Rhythm Waveform:** ECG/EKG-style waveforms. Used for Book 2 (Myocardial Chorus) branding.

**Sacred Geometry Overlay:** Flower of Life, Metatron's Cube, Sri Yantra patterns at 10-20% opacity as background.

---

### Graphic System Rules

- Use triangles as primary framing device (chapter headings, pull quotes)
- Layer geometric patterns at low opacity (10-30%) as background textures
- Combine organic (neural, cardiac) with geometric (triangles, hexagons) to represent biology + mysticism fusion
- Soma Gold used to "light up" key geometric elements, suggesting activation/coherence
